## Context

Qalqul Engine builds conversational AI and contact-centre software for banks, insurers, healthcare and retail in Morocco and Saudi Arabia. Clients needed bots that understood their customers, and integrations that fit into their own apps.

## My part

I worked on the conversational core and on everything clients embedded.

- Built NLP/NLU engines on language servers and Duckling, and worked on Botpress v12 core.
- Built no-code workflow systems and n8n integrations.
- Shipped portals, browser SDKs in TypeScript, Kotlin SDKs for Android, and React chat widgets over WebSockets.

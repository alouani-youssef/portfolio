## Context

Qalqul Engine builds conversational AI and contact-centre software for banks, insurers, healthcare and retail in Morocco and Saudi Arabia. Clients needed bots that understood their customers, embedded in their own websites and apps, and a contact centre that could hand those conversations to agents in real time.

## My part

I worked on the conversational core and on everything clients embedded in their own products.

- **Understanding**: NLP/NLU engines on language servers and Duckling, with intents and entities tuned for each client.
- **Chatbots**: public chatbots for client websites and apps, and work on Botpress v12 core. Chatbots are publicly exposed, so their security mattered as much as their features: who can open a session, how much traffic one client can send, and what personal data is kept.
- **Real-time platform**: services moved to stateless processes, with conversations in real time over WebSockets and work dispatched to the right agent.
- **Integrations**: portals, browser SDKs in TypeScript, Kotlin SDKs for Android, React chat widgets, no-code workflows and n8n.

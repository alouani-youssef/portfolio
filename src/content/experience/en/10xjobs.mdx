## Context

Software engineers lose hours a week scanning job boards for the few posts that fit. 10xJobs does that scanning for them and only shows posts that match a profile they publish.

## My part

I build 10xJobs end to end as a side project: a multi-user SaaS on top of Apify actors, and a pipeline that collects new posts, extracts their fields into a typed record with an LLM, and matches them to each user's published profiles. The pipeline is tuned for cost, efficiency and performance, since every scraped post and every match has a price.

## Context

Software engineers lose hours a week scanning job boards for the few posts that fit. 10xJobs does that scanning for them and only shows posts that match a profile they publish.

## My part

I founded 10xJobs and built it end to end: a multi-user SaaS on top of Apify actors, and a pipeline that collects new posts, extracts their fields and matches them to each registered user's published profile. The pipeline is tuned for cost, efficiency and performance, since every scraped post and every match has a price.

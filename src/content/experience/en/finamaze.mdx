## Context

Finamaze runs a robo-advisor that builds AI-tailored portfolios for each investor's risk profile. It began as a consumer app and moved to selling the platform to financial institutions, where onboarding regulated investors needs reliable identity checks and moving money needs reconciliation that never drifts.

## My part

I was a backend engineer through Finamaze's move from a B2C app to a multi-tenant B2B platform for financial institutions.

- **Migration**: each institution became a tenant with its own investors, data and configuration, and the infrastructure was adapted to keep tenants apart.
- **KYC**: document and visual verification in investor onboarding.
- **Reconciliation**: keeping the platform's ledger in step with the bank.
- **Robo-advisor**: backend work alongside the AI that builds portfolios for each risk profile.
- **Front ends**: React and Next.js.

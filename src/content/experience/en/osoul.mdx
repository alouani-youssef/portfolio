## Context

Fine-dining restaurants lose most of the data that would tell them who their guests are and what they come back for. Osoul is Revenue as a Service (RaaS): a SaaS growth engine built around specific revenue KPIs, with management tools built in, that captures that data with mature technologies and turns it into better KPIs.

## My part

I am the technical founder and own the platform end to end: the multi-tenant backend on NestJS, PostgreSQL, Redis and Keycloak, and the three front ends.

- **Ordering and sessions**: NFC/QR table ordering with token-based table sessions for anonymous diners.
- **Reservations**: deposits, queues, multi-channel reminders and bots, with loyalty used to smooth peak hours.
- **Recommendations**: dish and beverage recommendations and meal-completion promos.
- **Ad signals**: a tracking event schema and server-side CAPI events for ad-audience optimisation.
- **AI assistant**: LangChain, PII redaction, and the user's auth propagated to every tool call.
- **Identity and operations**: diner identity merging, POS/PMS integrations, occupancy and SLA tracking, event-based dynamic menu pricing.
- **Front ends**: Osoul Portal (web), Osoul Guest App and Osoul Waiter App (React Native).

See the [Osoul case study](/projects/osoul) for the architecture.

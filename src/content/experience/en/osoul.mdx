## Context

Fine-dining restaurants lose most of the data that would tell them who their guests are and what they come back for. Osoul is Revenue as a Service (RaaS): a SaaS growth engine built around specific revenue KPIs, with management tools built in, that captures that data and turns it into better KPIs.

## My part

I am the technical founder and own the platform end to end: the multi-tenant backend on NestJS, PostgreSQL, Redis and Keycloak, and the three front ends.

- **AI assistant**: LangChain tools that act with the user's own permissions, with personal data redacted and a confirmation before anything is written.
- **Recommendations**: dish and drink pairings, meal-completion promos and event-based dynamic menu pricing.
- **Ad signals**: a tracking event schema and signed server-side CAPI events for ad-audience optimisation.
- **Ordering and sessions**: NFC/QR table ordering with short-lived table sessions for anonymous diners.
- **Reservations**: deposits, queues, multi-channel reminders and bots, with loyalty used to smooth peak hours.
- **Operations**: diner identity merging, POS/PMS integrations, occupancy and SLA tracking.
- **Front ends**: Osoul Portal (web), Osoul Guest App and Osoul Waiter App (React Native).

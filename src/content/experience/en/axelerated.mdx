## Context

Axelerated Solutions builds Sowtek, an omnichannel customer-engagement platform for contact centres in Saudi Arabia: calls, WhatsApp, email and social media in one place, with AI on top. Its clients include government and security organisations, so privacy and deployment flexibility matter as much as features.

## My part

I was a founding engineer and the backend lead in a team of nine.

- Fine-tuned Whisper large-v3 for Saudi-dialect call transcription, with an audio pipeline for WhatsApp and Asterisk calls.
- Built RAG on Milvus, coding agents and tool-using chatbots.
- Designed the Customer Voice RAG pipeline, with an MCP server as its main retriever, and owned its architecture, auth, privacy and retention.
- Built an MCP server over our backend APIs, with every request validated through Keycloak.
- Built knowledge-base search over Sowtek's KB module with Docling and zero-shot classification.

### Migrations and deployments

- Business model: B2C to B2B.
- Architecture: stateful to stateless services.
- Orchestration: Docker Compose to Kubernetes.
- Hosting: Hetzner to Oracle Cloud.
- Hybrid deployments for private, banking and government institutions.

## Problem

Finamaze grew fast: onboarding, KYC, portfolios, payments and reconciliation lived close together in the backend, so a change in one area risked the others, and onboarding a bank partner meant touching everything. {/* CHECK: confirm the starting architecture */}

## Constraints

- **Live money**: investors keep trading and money keeps moving during the migration.
- **No big bang**: the migration happens in steps, each one shippable on its own.
- **Ledger correctness**: reconciliation must agree with the bank before and after every step.

## Key decisions

1. **Identity first.** Moving authentication to [Keycloak](/projects/finamaze-keycloak) removed the most shared concern before splitting anything else.
2. **Strangle, don't rewrite.** Each domain moved behind its boundary while the old paths kept serving traffic.
3. **Reconciliation as the safety net.** The ledger check proved after each step that no money moved differently.

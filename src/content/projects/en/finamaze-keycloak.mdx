## Problem

A robo-advisor holds investors' money and identity documents. Authentication, sessions and roles had to meet a bank partner's expectations and be the same across web, mobile and the back office, instead of being re-implemented in each service. {/* CHECK: starting point inferred */}

## Constraints

- **Regulated users**: investors only reach investing features after KYC, so identity state and roles drive what they can do.
- **Many clients**: web, iOS/Android and back-office tools share one identity.
- **Strong auth for staff**: back-office access needs stricter policies than investor access. {/* CHECK */}

## Key decisions

1. **One identity provider for every client.** Web, mobile and back office stop owning passwords and sessions.
2. **Roles follow onboarding.** Completing KYC is what grants investing roles, so access control and compliance stay in step.
3. **Services only validate tokens.** No service stores credentials or session state of its own.

## Problem

Contact-centre agents in Saudi Arabia answer customers on WhatsApp, email, X, Instagram and Facebook, often the same customer on several of them. Each channel has its own API, webhooks, message formats, rate limits and delivery rules. Agents need one inbox and one customer history, in real time.

## Constraints

- **Every channel is different**: webhooks, polling, media types, message windows and rate limits all vary. {/* CHECK: confirm channel list */}
- **Real time**: messages appear for the right agent immediately, with typing and delivery states.
- **No lost messages**: channel webhooks retry and duplicate, so ingestion must be idempotent.
- **Routing**: conversations go to the right queue and agent, and can be handed to bots and workflows.

## Key decisions

1. **One conversation model.** Every connector translates its channel into the same messages and conversations, so routing, history and analytics are written once.
2. **Connectors are isolated.** A channel API outage or change only touches its connector, not the inbox.
3. **Idempotent ingestion.** Webhook retries and duplicates are expected, so every message is keyed by its channel ID.
